Our approach

A small space.
High standards.

Bathroom renovations place several systems in close proximity. The sequence of demolition, surface preparation, waterproofing, tile and finishing must be considered as a coordinated scope.

Tile layout, grout lines, fixture alignment, transitions, ventilation and cleaning access matter to both appearance and performance. We emphasize careful decisions before work begins and regular review as it progresses.

Questions before you begin

What clients
often ask.

Every property and project is different. These answers offer a starting point for the conversation.

Do you work on bathrooms in NYC apartments?

We discuss bathroom remodeling for apartments and residences. Building regulations and any required permissions are reviewed for each project.

Can I keep my bathroom layout?

Keeping the existing layout may simplify parts of a project, but the right solution depends on the condition of the space and your goals.

Do you provide plumbing or electrical work?

Work requiring licensed plumbers or electricians must be performed by the appropriate licensed professionals. We can discuss coordination as part of the overall project.

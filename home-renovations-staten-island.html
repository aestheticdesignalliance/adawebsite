Our approach

A clear vision.
Every detail considered.

Home renovations bring together many decisions: what should change, what needs to stay, how rooms connect, and what matters most for daily life. We start by listening, then help shape a practical scope around those priorities.

Details such as transitions between flooring, alignment of trim, consistency of finishes, and coordination between trades influence how a finished space feels. We look at those details as part of the whole, not as an afterthought.

Questions before you begin

What clients
often ask.

Every property and project is different. These answers offer a starting point for the conversation.

Do you renovate both houses and apartments on Staten Island?

We discuss residential renovations for houses and apartments. Building requirements and the scope of each project determine the appropriate approach.

Can you help with multiple rooms in one renovation?

Yes. We can discuss a coordinated scope across multiple areas and construction management for scheduling, materials and trades.

Will my renovation require permits?

Some changes do. Permit requirements depend on the work and building. Where required, the appropriate registered or licensed professionals must be involved.

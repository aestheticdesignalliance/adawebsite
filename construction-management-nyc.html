Questions before you begin

What clients
often ask.

Every property and project is different. These answers offer a starting point for the conversation.

Is construction management the same as general contracting?

No. Construction management is a service focused on planning, coordination and oversight. Project-specific contracting, permits and licensed trade requirements must be addressed separately.

Can you assist before construction starts?

Yes. Early planning may include scope definition, sequencing, procurement considerations and coordination needs.

Do you manage both residential and commercial work?

We can discuss management needs across different types of projects. Final scope and responsibilities are agreed based on the property and requirements.

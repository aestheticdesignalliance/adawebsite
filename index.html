Have another question? Get in touch ↗
What kinds of projects does Prime Build take on?

We focus on home renovations and remodeling, kitchens, bathrooms, exterior improvements, storefront and commercial improvement projects, and construction management. We evaluate each project individually to confirm the right scope, licenses and specialists.

Is Prime Build licensed in NYC?

Yes. Prime Build operates under NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP) Home Improvement Contractor License No. 2126717-DCWP. Verify the license with NYC DCWP ↗.

Can you coordinate a complete renovation?

Yes. Our construction management services can help coordinate scope, schedules, budgets, trades and quality expectations. Work requiring particular registrations, permits or separately licensed professionals is addressed based on the project.

Where does Prime Build work?

We serve clients in New York City. Contact us with your borough, neighborhood and project details so we can confirm availability for your location.

How do I start a project?

Call or email us with the property location, a short description of the work, your desired timeline and any available photos or drawings. We will discuss the scope and appropriate next steps.

Questions before you begin

What clients
often ask.

Every property and project is different. These answers offer a starting point for the conversation.

Do exterior improvements in NYC require permits?

Many exterior and facade projects do. Requirements depend on the building and scope; the appropriate registered design professionals and licensed contractors must be involved where required.

Can you help with facade and cladding coordination?

We can discuss coordination of materials, interfaces, sequencing and project management for cladding-related work.

Do you work on storefronts?

We can evaluate storefront and commercial improvement inquiries and discuss any necessary approvals and specialist trades.
